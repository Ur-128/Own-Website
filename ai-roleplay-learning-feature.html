AI Roleplay Learning Feature

Helping users practice communication with AI simulations.

Role: Assistant Product Manager · Focus: Feature discovery, engagement, and learning outcomes

Problem

Learners wanted a safe space to practice real conversations, but existing content was static and didn't adapt to their responses or skill level.

What I looked at

  • Usage patterns on existing learning modules and practice tools.
  • Feedback from users who struggled to transfer theory into real conversations.
  • Competitive landscape for AI-powered practice or coaching tools.

Approach

  • Defined the core user jobs-to-be-done for the roleplay feature.
  • Partnered with design and engineering to scope an MVP that felt realistic but was technically feasible.
  • Planned experiments around prompt design, scenario templates, and feedback loops.
