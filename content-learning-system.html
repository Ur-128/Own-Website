Content Learning System

Structuring learning journeys using video + PDF modules.

Role: Assistant Product Manager · Focus: Curriculum structure & learner progression

Problem

Content was available, but learners didn't know what to do next. Progression through videos, PDFs, and exercises felt fragmented, leading to drop-offs and incomplete journeys.

What I looked at

  • Completion rates across different content types and modules.
  • Existing "playlists" or informal paths learners created for themselves.
  • Qualitative feedback on where learners felt lost or overwhelmed.

Approach

  • Defined clear learning paths with milestones and outcomes for each module.
  • Introduced lightweight progress indicators and reminders to pull users back into the journey.
  • Collaborated with content and design to align narrative, sequencing, and UI.
